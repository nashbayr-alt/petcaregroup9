How adoption works

Three steps from first look to first night at home.

  1. Browse

    Look through our dogs and cats and shortlist the ones that fit your home.

  2. Apply

    Fill in the short adoption form with your contact details and why you’d like the pet.

  3. Meet and take home

    We contact you, set up a meeting, and finalise the adoption once you’re both happy.

How booking and payment work

  1. Log in

    Create a free account or log in with the button at the top. Log out from the same place.

  2. Choose a service

    Press Book on a service, then enter your pet’s details, a date and a time slot.

  3. Pick how to pay

    Pay at the clinic, or choose card, GCash, Maya or PayPal.

  4. Manage it

    See, pay for or cancel bookings under My bookings.

Everyday care basics

Simple habits that keep a new pet well.

  • Food
    Feed a diet made for your pet’s species and age, and keep fresh water out all day.
  • Grooming
    Brush regularly, bathe with pet-safe shampoo, and use wipes between baths.
  • Play
    Daily walks for dogs and toys for cats keep them fit and stop boredom.
  • Vet visits
    Book a first check-up soon after adoption and keep vaccinations up to date.
